Questions

What is Halcyon?

Halcyon: Calm Puzzle Games is a free Android app with about eighty brain games in one place, including Sudoku, Nonogram, Kakuro, Chess, Mahjong Solitaire, Klondike, Jigsaw and Tangram, in a calm glass style.

Is Halcyon free?

Yes. Halcyon is free to download and play on Google Play. It is supported by ads and needs no account.

Does Halcyon work offline?

Yes. The games work without an internet connection, so you can play on a flight or the commute.

Does Halcyon have timers or lives?

No. There are no countdown timers and no lives system. You can take as long as you like on any puzzle.

Which Sudoku variants does Halcyon include?

Classic Sudoku plus Sudoku X, Killer Sudoku, Kakuro, Futoshiki, Calcudoku and Suguru, alongside other logic puzzles such as Nonogram, Queens and Star Battle.

Is Halcyon available on iPhone?

Not yet. Halcyon is on Android through Google Play; an iPhone version is planned for later.
