Halcyon Privacy Policy

Halcyon: Calm Puzzle Games · Last updated: 19 November 2026

Halcyon ("the app") is a collection of puzzle and brain games published on Google Play and the App Store by A2Z AIRPORT PARKING LTD ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information is handled when you use the app.

1. The short version

Halcyon does not collect, store, sell or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no trackers, and the app works without an internet connection.

2. Information the app stores on your device

Your puzzle progress, points, level, and your sound and reminder settings are saved only on your own device, using the operating system's standard app storage. We do not run accounts or servers for Halcyon, and this data is never sent to us. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

3. Notifications

If you turn on the optional evening reminder, the app schedules a local notification on your device. No server is involved and no notification data is sent to us. You can turn the reminder off in the app or in your device's notification settings at any time.

4. Sharing

When you tap Share after a puzzle, the app hands a short message (for example "I solved Sudoku on Halcyon") to the app you choose. Nothing is sent unless you send it yourself, and Halcyon does not see or keep what you share. That app's own privacy policy applies.

5. Children

Halcyon collects no information from anyone, including children under 13.

6. Security and data deletion

We do not hold any of your personal data. To delete your game data, clear the app's storage or uninstall the app.

7. Changes

We may update this policy. The "Last updated" date above shows the latest version.
