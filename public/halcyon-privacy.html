Halcyon Privacy Policy

Halcyon: Calm Puzzle Games · Last updated: 20 November 2026

Halcyon ("the app") is a collection of puzzle and brain games published on Google Play and the App Store by A2Z AIRPORT PARKING LTD ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information is handled when you use the app.

1. The short version

Halcyon has no accounts and we do not run any servers for it, so we never receive your personal data ourselves. From version 1.0.1 the Android app shows ads served by Google AdMob, which helps keep Halcyon free. Google, as the ad provider, collects some device information to show, measure and limit ads, as described in section 3. Your puzzle progress never leaves your device.

2. Information the app stores on your device

Your puzzle progress, points, level, hint allowance, and your sound and reminder settings are saved only on your own device, using the operating system's standard app storage. This data is never sent to us. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows banner ads, an occasional full-screen ad between puzzles, and optional video ads you can choose to watch for extra hints. These ads are provided by Google AdMob. To serve them, Google may collect and process:

  • device identifiers, including your device's advertising ID;
  • your IP address and approximate (not precise) location derived from it;
  • basic device and app information, such as device model, operating system version and app version;
  • ad interaction data, such as which ads were shown or tapped, and diagnostic data such as crashes of the ads component.

Google uses this data to deliver ads, measure their performance, prevent fraud and, where you allow it, personalise the ads you see. Ads in Halcyon are limited to content suitable for general audiences. You can read how Google uses this information at How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services, and Google's own privacy policy at policies.google.com/privacy.

4. Your choices about ads

  • Consent in the UK, EEA and Switzerland. If you are in these regions, the app asks for your consent before any personalised ads are shown, using Google's consent form. You can consent, decline, or manage individual options. You can change your choice at any time with the Privacy choices button at the top of the library screen.
  • Advertising ID. On Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ads personalisation, in your device's Settings under Google › Ads (or Privacy › Ads).
  • US state privacy rights. Where applicable, you can opt out of the use of your data for targeted advertising through your device's ad settings described above.

Video ads for hints are always optional; you are never required to watch one to keep playing.

5. Notifications

If you turn on the optional evening reminder, the app schedules a local notification on your device. No server is involved and no notification data is sent to us. You can turn the reminder off in the app or in your device's notification settings at any time.

6. Sharing

When you tap Share after a puzzle, the app hands a short message (for example "I solved Sudoku on Halcyon") to the app you choose. Nothing is sent unless you send it yourself, and Halcyon does not see or keep what you share. That app's own privacy policy applies.

7. Children

Halcyon is intended for a general audience aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children, and ads in the app are restricted to content suitable for general audiences.

8. Security, retention and deletion

We do not hold any of your personal data. To delete your game data, clear the app's storage or uninstall the app. Data collected by Google for advertising is retained and deleted under Google's policies; you can reset your advertising ID at any time as described above.

9. Changes

We may update this policy. The "Last updated" date above shows the latest version.
