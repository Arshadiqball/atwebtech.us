The best starting words for 5-letter word games

Short answer: start with STARE, SLATE or RAISE. If your first guess shows little, follow with COULD, which shares no letters with STARE.

We counted the letters in the 1,500 common 5-letter words that WordGlow uses in its Medium level and scored every word by how much it tells you. A good first guess uses five different letters, picks the most common ones, and puts them where they most often appear.

A simple two-guess plan

  1. Play STARE. It tests the five most useful letters in their most likely spots.
  2. If you get one hit or none, play COULD (or CHILD). Together the two guesses test ten different letters.
  3. From guess three, only play words that fit every clue so far: keep green letters in place and move yellow letters to a new spot.

What about 4, 6 and 7-letter words?

The same idea works at every length: use different letters, favour E, A, R, T, O, I, L and S, and avoid repeating a letter until you have to. WordGlow has levels for 4, 5, 6 and 7-letter words if you want to practise.
