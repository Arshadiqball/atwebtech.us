10 games like Wordle

Finished today's Wordle and want more? These ten free word games each change the formula in a different way. One of them, WordGlow, is ours, so we put it first and say so; the rest are games we think are worth your time.

1. WordGlow

Wordle rules plus four word lengths (4 to 7 letters), unlimited play, a 90-second timed mode, and the meaning of every answer saved to a review list. Best for: Players who want more than one puzzle a day and want to learn the words. (This is our game.)

2. Quordle

Four 5-letter words at once. Each guess applies to all four boards, and you get 9 guesses. Best for: Players who find one board too easy.

3. Octordle

Eight boards at once with 13 guesses. Best for: Quordle fans who want a longer challenge.

4. Dordle

Two words side by side with 7 guesses. Best for: A gentle step up from Wordle.

5. Absurdle

An adversarial version: the game changes its secret word to avoid your guesses for as long as it can. Unlimited guesses. Best for: Logic lovers who enjoy cornering the game.

6. Waffle

A grid of six crossing words. You swap letters into place with a limited number of swaps. Best for: People who like anagrams and crosswords.

7. Connections

Sort 16 words into four hidden groups of four. Four mistakes and you are out. Best for: Players who prefer meaning and wordplay to spelling.

8. Spelling Bee

Make as many words as you can from seven letters, always using the centre letter. Best for: A slower, longer daily puzzle.

9. Wordscapes

Swipe letters on a wheel to fill a crossword-style grid across thousands of levels. Best for: Relaxed play without a daily limit.

10. Words With Friends

A Scrabble-style board game played against friends or strangers. Best for: Competitive players who like turn-based matches.

Which one should you pick?

  • More Wordle-style puzzles per day: WordGlow's levels, or Quordle.
  • To build vocabulary: WordGlow (meanings after every round) or Spelling Bee.
  • A harder logic challenge: Absurdle or Octordle.
  • Something different from guessing letters: Connections or Waffle.
  • To play against people: Words With Friends.
