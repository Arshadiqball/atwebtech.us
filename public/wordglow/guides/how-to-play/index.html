How to play WordGlow

WordGlow is a word-guessing game for Android. You have 6 tries to find a hidden word, and coloured tiles tell you how close each guess is. When you finish, you learn what the word means.

The rules in four steps

  1. Type a word and press Enter. Any real word of the right length counts as a guess. Words that are not in the dictionary, or that you already tried, are not accepted and do not use up a try.
  2. Read the colours. Green: the letter is in the word and in the right spot. Yellow: the letter is in the word but in a different spot. Grey: the letter is not in the word.
  3. Use the clues for your next guess. Keep green letters where they are, move yellow letters to a new position and avoid grey letters.
  4. Solve it in 6 tries or fewer. When the round ends, WordGlow shows the answer with its part of speech and meaning and saves it to your My Words list.

Game modes

Daily puzzle

One 5-letter word per day, the same for every player in the world. Solving it on consecutive days builds your streak.

Play by level

Unlimited puzzles at four difficulties, each with 1,500 words that never repeat:

LevelWord lengthPoints bonus
Easy4 letters×1.0
Medium5 letters×1.25
Hard6 letters×1.5
Advanced7 letters×2.0

Beat the Clock

You get 90 seconds per word. If time runs out you can watch a short optional ad for 30 more seconds, up to three times per word.

Points, ranks and streaks

You earn more points for solving in fewer tries, solving quickly and not using hints, and harder levels multiply your score. Points move you up ten ranks, from Rookie to Mythic. Your streak counts the days in a row you have solved the daily puzzle.

Hints

If you are stuck you can reveal a letter. Inviting a friend with the Challenge button gives you 3 free hints for the day; otherwise a hint costs a short optional ad.

Five tips to solve faster

  1. Open with a word that has five different, common letters, such as STARE or SLATE. See the best starting words.
  2. Use your second guess to test new letters instead of repeating grey ones.
  3. Remember that a letter can appear twice, as in LEVEL or SPEED.
  4. Think about common endings: -ER, -ED, -LY, -ING and -TION for longer words.
  5. Stuck between several answers? Guess a word that tests the letters that differ.
