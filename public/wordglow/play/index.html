Questions

How do I play?

Guess the hidden 5-letter word in 6 tries. After each guess the tiles change colour: green means the letter is in the right spot, yellow means it is in the word but in a different spot, and dark means it is not in the word.

Is it free? Do I need an account?

It is free, with no account and no download. You can play as many puzzles as you like.

What is the difference between this and the WordGlow app?

The browser version is unlimited 5-letter practice. The free Android app adds a daily word shared by all players, levels with 4, 6 and 7-letter words, a 90-second timed mode, points, ranks, streaks and a saved list of the words you have learned.

Is this Wordle?

No. WordGlow is a separate game that uses the same familiar green and yellow clue style, with its own word list and a meaning shown after every puzzle.
