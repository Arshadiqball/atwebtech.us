In one sentence

WordGlow is a free word puzzle for Android where every answer teaches you its meaning.

About

WordGlow is a guess-the-word game: you have 6 tries to find a hidden word, and coloured tiles show which letters are right. When the round ends, the game shows the word's part of speech and meaning and saves it to a personal "My Words" list.

It was built by a solo developer who liked daily word games but noticed they taught nothing: you guess an unusual word and forget it a minute later. WordGlow keeps the familiar format and adds the meaning, more word lengths and unlimited play.

Alongside the Android app there is a free browser version with unlimited 5-letter puzzles, plus a solver and an unscrambler that also show word meanings.

Features

  • A daily word shared by all players, 6 tries
  • Four levels: 4, 5, 6 and 7-letter words, 6,000 words that never repeat
  • The meaning of every answer (from WordNet 3.0), saved to "My Words"
  • Beat the Clock: 90 seconds per word
  • Points, 10 ranks, daily streaks and shareable score cards
  • Works offline, no account
