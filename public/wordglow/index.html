How to play

  1. Type any real word and press Enter.
  2. Green = right letter, right spot. Yellow = in the word, wrong spot. Grey = not in the word.
  3. Use the clues to find the word in 6 tries or fewer.
  4. Learn its meaning, share your score, and come back tomorrow for a new one!

Questions & answers

What is WordGlow?

WordGlow is a free daily word puzzle game for Android. You guess a hidden word in 6 tries, and coloured tiles show which letters are correct. After each puzzle the game shows the word's meaning, so you learn a new word every day.

Is WordGlow free?

Yes. WordGlow is free to download and play on Google Play. It is supported by ads, and optional rewarded ads give extras such as a hint or 30 more seconds.

How is WordGlow different from Wordle?

WordGlow has the same guess-in-6-tries idea, and adds four levels (4, 5, 6 and 7-letter words), unlimited play beyond the daily puzzle, a 90-second Beat the Clock mode, points and ranks, and the meaning of every answer saved to a personal My Words list.

Can WordGlow help me learn English vocabulary?

Yes. Every answer comes with its part of speech and meaning, and all the words you solve are saved in My Words so you can review them. The Easy level uses common 4-letter words, and Advanced uses 7-letter words.

How many words are in WordGlow?

The levels contain 6,000 words (1,500 each for 4, 5, 6 and 7 letters) that do not repeat, plus a separate daily puzzle that is the same for every player.

Does WordGlow work offline?

Yes. Puzzles, levels and word meanings work without an internet connection, and no account is needed.

Where can I download WordGlow?

WordGlow is on Google Play for Android phones and tablets: search for "WordGlow: Daily Word Puzzle" or visit atwebtech.us/wordglow. An iPhone version is not available yet.
