Wordle solver

Stuck on today's puzzle? Enter the clues you have and this free solver lists every word that still fits, with the best next guesses at the top and the meaning of each common word.

How to use it

  1. Pick the word length of your puzzle.
  2. Type each green letter into the box for its position.
  3. Type each yellow letter into the box for the position where you tried it. That tells the solver the letter is in the word but not there.
  4. Type all grey letters into the last box, then press Find words.

Want to get better without a solver? Read the best starting words, or practise with unlimited free puzzles.

Questions

How does the Wordle solver work?

It checks about 53,000 English words against your clues. A word stays on the list only if it has your green letters in the right spots, contains every yellow letter somewhere else, and uses none of the grey letters.

How are the results ranked?

Common everyday words come first, because puzzle answers are almost always common words. Within each group, words whose letters appear most often among the remaining candidates are ranked higher, so the top guesses rule out the most options.

Does it work for games other than Wordle?

Yes. Choose 4, 5, 6 or 7 letters to use it with WordGlow levels and other guess-the-word games that use green and yellow clues.

Is the solver free?

Yes. It is free, needs no account, and runs in your browser. The letters you type are not sent anywhere.
