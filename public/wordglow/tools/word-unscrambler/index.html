Word unscrambler

Type your jumbled letters and get every word you can make from them, longest first. Common words show their meaning, so you learn the word as well as find it.

How it works

The unscrambler compares your letters with about 53,000 English words of 4 to 7 letters and keeps the ones that can be spelled from them. Results are grouped by length, and within each group the everyday words come first.

Looking for words with a fixed start or ending instead? Use the word finder.

Questions

How do I unscramble letters into words?

Type your letters in any order and press Unscramble. The tool lists every word of 4 to 7 letters that can be spelled using only those letters, each letter used no more times than you entered it.

Can I use a blank or wildcard?

Yes. Type a question mark (?) for each blank tile. A blank can stand for any letter.

Which word games is it useful for?

Anagram puzzles, jumbles, letter-wheel games, crosswords and Scrabble-style games. Check your own game's dictionary, because accepted words differ between games.

Why do some words show a meaning and others do not?

About 6,000 common words come with a short meaning from WordNet. Rarer words are listed without one.
