**Tagline (60 characters):** Plan, schedule and publish your social media in one place

**One sentence:** atwebtech is a social media scheduler for small businesses, creators and agencies: one calendar for eight networks, an AI assistant for first drafts, one inbox for comments, and a free plan for three channels.

**Short description (about 250 characters):**
Plan a week of posts in one sitting. atwebtech gives you one calendar for Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn, YouTube, Pinterest, Threads, Bluesky and Mastodon, an AI assistant that drafts with you, one inbox for comments, and insights on what works. Free for 3 channels.

**Long description:**
atwebtech is a social media workspace for small businesses, creators and agencies.

- Plan and publish: write a post once, send it to several channels, and let it go out on time. Drafts, repeating posts and evergreen posts that recycle themselves.
- Write faster: an AI assistant drafts a post from a short brief, and a Trending list (YouTube, Google Search, Reddit, Hacker News) gives you something to start from.
- Answer your community: one inbox for comments from every connected channel, with saved replies and AI-suggested replies.
- Work as a team: send posts for approval, and switch between your own workspace and client workspaces.
- Know what works: followers, views, reactions and comments in one place, with your best times to post.
- Automations: turn new blog posts into social posts, and have next week planned for you.

It runs in the browser and on iPhone and iPad. It is free for up to 3 channels with no credit card; paid plans are billed per channel.

**Tags:** social media scheduler, social media management, content calendar, Instagram scheduler, AI writing, small business, creators, agencies

## 2. Communities

These are read by people, not listed by a form. Post one a day at most, from a real account, and stay to answer.

### Show HN (news.ycombinator.com/submit)
- Title: `Show HN: Atwebtech – a social media scheduler billed per channel, free for 3`
- URL: https://atwebtech.us
- First comment:

> I built this for small businesses that post to a handful of networks and do not want a $99 a month tool.
>
> It schedules posts to Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn, YouTube, Pinterest, Threads, Bluesky and Mastodon from one queue, with an inbox for comments and basic analytics. There is an AI assistant for first drafts, and a Trending list that pulls from YouTube, Google Trends, Reddit and the HN API to give you something to start from.
>
> Stack: plain PHP and MySQL on the server with no framework, server-rendered pages, and a Flutter app for iPhone and iPad. Each network's publishing API has its own surprises; happy to go into any of them.
>
> Free for 3 channels, no card. Paid plans are per channel. I would like to hear where it falls short.

HN rules: no marketing language in the title, do not ask anyone to upvote, and answer questions plainly.
