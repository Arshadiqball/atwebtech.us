atwebtech is a social media scheduler for small businesses, creators and agencies who want to plan a week of posts in one sitting instead of posting at the last minute.

## One calendar for eight networks

Write a post once and send it to Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn, YouTube, Pinterest, Threads, Bluesky and Mastodon. Each network's limits are shown as you type, and you can preview the post before it goes out. Drafts, repeating posts and evergreen posts that recycle themselves keep the queue full.

## Help with what to write

An AI assistant drafts a post from a short brief, and you stay the editor: nothing is published without you. A Trending list shows what people are watching, reading and searching this week on YouTube, Google Search, Reddit and Hacker News, so you always have something to start from.

## One inbox and clear numbers

Comments from every connected channel land in one inbox, with saved replies for common questions. Insights show followers, reactions and comments in one place, along with your best times to post.

## Built for small teams

Send posts for approval, and switch between your own workspace and client workspaces. Automations can turn new blog posts into social posts and plan next week for you.

## Pricing

atwebtech runs in the browser and on iPhone and iPad. It is free for up to 3 channels with no credit card. Paid plans are billed per channel, from $6 a month, so you only pay for what you connect.
