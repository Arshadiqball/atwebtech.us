# atwebtech growth strategy (US launch)

Modeled on what Metricool does publicly (researched 9 Nov 2026), adapted to atwebtech's size and budget. Metricool says it has "over 4 million professionals"; it does not publish daily sign-up numbers, so treat any "hundreds a day" figure as a goal to grow into, not a benchmark.

## What Metricool does that works

| Lever | Metricool | atwebtech today | Do next |
|---|---|---|---|
| Free forever plan, one CTA everywhere | "Create your free account" | Free plan + "Start free" everywhere | Done |
| Low price vs big rivals | From $20/mo vs Hootsuite $99 | From $5/channel/mo (yearly) | Add comparison pages (vs Buffer, Hootsuite, Later, Metricool) |
| Free tools as lead magnets | Hashtag generators, engagement calculator | Trending ideas, AI writer, Link in Bio (inside the app) | Add 2-3 public free tools (hashtag generator, caption generator, best-time-to-post) |
| Education | Free courses, webinars, yearly reports | 4 guides | Publish 2 guides a week + a monthly "US social media calendar" download |
| Affiliate program | 25% of subscriptions, instant, no approval | None | Launch once Stripe is live: 25% recurring for 12 months |
| Agencies / approvals | Approval system, white label | Approvals + client review link | Agency landing page using approvals |
| Social proof | User count, brand logos, G2 badge | None yet (don't fake it) | Collect 10 real testimonials from first users, then add G2/Capterra listings |

## Paid acquisition (Meta) — first 30 days

1. **Week 1 (now):** Leads campaign, US, $20/day, optimize for Complete registration. 3 creatives (Plan a week, 7 networks, Stop posting last minute) × formats (1:1, 4:5, 9:16 + Reels video). Let Meta pick the winner.
2. **Week 2:** Pause the weakest creative. Raise budget 20% on the winner every 3 days while cost per sign-up stays under your target.
3. **Week 3:** Retargeting ad set: people who visited the site or pricing but didn't sign up (Pixel audiences), $5/day.
4. **Week 4:** Lookalike audience (1%) from people who signed up, once you have 100+ sign-ups.
5. **When Stripe is live:** switch the main campaign to optimize for Purchase once there are ~50 purchases a week.

Targets to watch in the admin CRM (Dashboard → Sources → "Meta ads"): cost per sign-up, sign-up → activated (connected a channel) rate, and activated → customer rate.

## Conversion basics (on the site)

- One CTA: "Start free". Done on every page.
- Sign-up in one step; first channel connected within 2 minutes (the onboarding already asks for focus channels).
- Upgrade prompts at the moment of need: channel limit, 10 scheduled posts, 100 ideas (built).
- Send a 5-email welcome series (day 0, 1, 3, 7, 14) once outbound email is set up.
