WordGlow Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

WordGlow ("the app") is a daily word puzzle game published on Google Play by A2Z AIRPORT PARKING LTD ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information is handled when you use the app.

1. Information the app stores on your device

Your game progress is saved only on your own device. This includes your scores, streaks, statistics, learned words and settings. We do not run accounts or servers for WordGlow, and this data is never sent to us. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

WordGlow is free and supported by ads served by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect and process information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, approximate location derived from it, and how you interact with ads. Google's use of this data is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Where required by law (for example in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland), the app asks for your consent before personalized ads are shown, using Google's consent form. You can reset or opt out of the advertising ID in your device settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads).

Rewarded videos are always optional. You choose to watch one in exchange for a hint, extra time, an extra try, more practice words or saving a streak.

3. Notifications

If you turn on daily reminders, they are scheduled on your device. No notification data is sent to us. You can turn reminders off in the app's Settings or in your device's notification settings at any time.

4. Sharing

When you use "Challenge friends" or "Share", the app opens the app you choose (for example WhatsApp) with a pre-written message or score image. Nothing is sent unless you send it yourself.

5. Children

WordGlow is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

6. Security and data deletion

We do not hold any of your personal data. To delete your game data, clear the app's storage or uninstall the app. For data processed by Google for advertising, see Google's controls at My Ad Center.

7. Changes

We may update this policy. The "Last updated" date above shows the latest version.
